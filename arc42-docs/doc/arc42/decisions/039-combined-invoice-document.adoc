[[adr-039]]
= ADR-039: Combined Invoice Document

== Status
Accepted. Supersedes <<adr-032,ADR-032>> where that decision removed the receipt pages from the PDF.

PO approval (visible output): 2026-10-12 receipt pages behind the invoice pages, header band
"Anlage n/m – <Dateiname>" and "Pos. <Nr>" on each receipt page, draft mark on receipt pages,
BT-123 carries the receipt description, an unreadable receipt fails the export; 2026-10-12 display
size of receipt pages (original size, at most the A4 print area).

== Context
<<adr-032,ADR-032>> carried every receipt only as Base64 in the e-invoice XML (BG-24) and removed the
receipt pages from the visible PDF. A reader of the PDF alone, which is the document that is
printed, filed and mailed, then saw an invoice that refers to supporting documents it does not
show, and the receipt was not recognisable as an attachment of the invoice at all. Separately,
`MustangTransactionBuilder` skipped a receipt it could not load with a log line, so a document
could be produced that silently lacked a receipt it was assigned.

== Decision
An invoice document is the invoice pages followed by the pages of every assigned receipt.

* *Pages*: each distinct receipt is appended behind the invoice pages, in one fixed order
  (`orderedDistinctReceipts` in `kotlin-shared`): invoice-level receipts before item-level ones,
  each in list order, a receipt attached more than once appears once (identity: object key, file
  name if there is none). The visible pages and the XML attachments take their list from this one
  function. Applies to drafts, issued invoices and credit notes alike.
* *Header band*: every receipt page carries "Anlage n/m – <file name>" and, for receipts attached to
  positions, "Pos. <number>", drawn as a text layer with an embedded font on top of the image
  (extractable, veraPDF-clean), not rasterised into it.
* *Raster only*: receipt pages are rendered to JPEG (`ReceiptAnnexBuilder`, `xpdf.receipt-annex.dpi`,
  150 dpi, quality 0.75, the resolution upload-time reduction already uses). A foreign PDF is never
  merged, so its fonts and colour spaces cannot break PDF/A-3 conformance and its defects stay
  isolated.
* *Display size*: a receipt page is shown in its original size, top-centred below the header band
  on an A4 page of matching orientation; it is never enlarged, so a till receipt prints at its real
  width. Only a page larger than the A4 print area is shrunk to fit; such sizes come from faulty
  page sizes (a photo converted at 72 dpi becomes a 1.4 m page), since receipts are A4 or smaller.
  The 150 dpi refer to this display size, which also bounds the bitmap of any page to the A4 raster
  (about 1240 × 1754 px) without a notice to the user. Upload reduction (`PdfBoxReceiptPdfReducer`)
  computes effective resolution against the same display size (`ReceiptDisplaySize`,
  `kotlin-shared`), so such a photo is reduced instead of rasterised into a larger file.
* *XML unchanged*: every receipt exactly once as BG-24 (BT-122 file name, BT-125 Base64); BT-123
  `Name` is the receipt description, the file name if there is none (`ReceiptDto.description`, an
  additive field of the job contract).
* *One embedded file*: the PDF/A-3 document still embeds only the XML.
* *Failure instead of skipping*: a receipt that cannot be read or rendered makes the export fail
  with a user-facing message (`ReceiptUnavailableException`), for pages and for XML alike.
* *Stamp*: the draft, storno and brand marks are stamped on every page of the finished
  document, receipt pages included, so a draft cannot be separated from its receipt pages.
* *Budget*: `CombinedDocumentSizeEstimator` sizes the document as reserve plus, per receipt, its Base64
  text and its rasterised pages (about the receipt size again, measured factor 0.98 for scan-like
  receipts). The upload warning and the export backstop `InvoiceDocumentTooLargeException`
  stay.

=== Alternatives Considered
Merge the original receipt pages instead of rasterising::
Rejected: veraPDF flags merged foreign pages as not PDF/A-3B conformant (non-embedded fonts).

Keep ADR-032 (XML only)::
Rejected: the PDF is the document people read; without the pages it does not show its own
supporting documents.

Embed the receipt additionally as a PDF/A-3 file::
Rejected: a third copy with no reader that needs it.

== Consequences
* The PDF now shows every receipt, in the same order as the XML lists them.
* Document size: receipts now count twice (Base64 in the XML, pages in the PDF). Measured with 19
  scan-like receipts: at 768 KiB each 32.8 MiB, above the 20 MiB limit of <<adr-014,ADR-014>>.
  Product decision 2026-10-12: the target size per receipt drops to 440 KiB and the 20 MiB limit
  stays; the reserve for the invoice body drops from 1 MiB to 512 KiB. Measured at 440 KiB: receipts
  7.8 MiB, pages in the PDF 7.7 MiB, Base64 in the XML 10.4 MiB, together 18.1 MiB; the budget
  estimate for the same set is 19.5 MiB. At 440 KiB the estimate with a 1 MiB reserve would be
  20.05 MiB and refuse the 19th receipt.
* A receipt that is no longer readable now blocks the export instead of being left out.
* Readability hint on upload: a dialog warns when a picked scan looks too coarse or blurred to read in
  print; it never blocks the upload. PO-Freigabe (Datum): 2026-10-12
* Proposed raster default 120 dpi / JPEG quality 0.75 instead of 150 dpi (smallest configuration that
  passes the OCR and SSIM gate on the synthetic receipts; A4 scan 86 KiB instead of 120 KiB). This
  changes the visible receipt pages. It is pending PO approval and is NOT yet applied to print:
  the annex still renders at 150 dpi (`xpdf.receipt-annex.dpi`). PO 2026-10-12: stays at 150 dpi; 120 dpi is reconsidered after the full stress run.
