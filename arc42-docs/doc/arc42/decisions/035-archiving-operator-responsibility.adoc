
[[adr-035]]
= ADR-035: Archiving Is the Operator's Responsibility

== Status
Accepted, 2026-10-07

== Context
Invoices must be kept for 8 years, books and annual statements for 10 years. The application is a pure invoicing program: issued invoices
are handed over to the business's bookkeeping or tax advisor. It is not a document archive (<<adr-034,ADR-034>>): there is no bucket versioning, no
retention and no archive package that counts as an independent copy. Invoices are normally
dispatched by e-mail, so the recipient-bound copy exists in the mail path as well.

== Decision
Archiving and retention are the operator's responsibility. Sent invoices, including the XML of
the e-invoice, are stored in the bookkeeping or archive system. Because invoices are sent by
e-mail, an e-mail archive that stores mail immutably is recommended. The application keeps its
document store for operation and for the recovery described in ADR-034, not as a retention
medium.

== Consequences
* The operator must archive every sent invoice with its XML outside the application.
* An immutable e-mail archive covers the dispatched copy; invoices not dispatched by mail must
  reach the archive or bookkeeping system by another route.
* Backups of the application database and object store are an operational safeguard, not an
  archive.
* Data access during a tax audit is provided by that archive or the bookkeeping
  system; the application delivers XML and PDF.
* Bookkeeping, DATEV export, payment tracking, dunning and receiving incoming e-invoices are out
  of scope of the application.
